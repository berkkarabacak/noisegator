This site

This marketing site does not use accounts, analytics, or tracking cookies of its own. We do not set cookies. GitHub Pages (and GitHub, if you follow a download or repo link) may log visits under their own policies.

The app

NoiseGator is a local Windows microphone noise gate. Audio stays on your PC. The app does not create an account and does not send your voice to the cloud.

Downloads

The Windows exe and source zip are hosted on GitHub. Fetching them is a GitHub request, not a request to a separate store or payment provider.
